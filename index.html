Tea questions

Good to know about TDG Tea

Does your tea contain caffeine?

Not at all. All TDG Tea products are completely caffeine-free (100% caffeine-free), making them suitable for daily consumption, even in the evening or for those sensitive to caffeine.

Does your tea contain added sugar or preservatives?

Absolutely not. All of our teas are made from natural herbs with no added sugar, preservatives, or artificial flavorings, strictly adhering to our “Sugar-Free Tea Enjoyment” principle.

Where are your ingredients sourced from?

Our herbs and Shan Tuyet tea are harvested from the northern highlands of Vietnam, including Phinh Ho, a region renowned for its pristine air, ancient tea trees, and native medicinal plants.
